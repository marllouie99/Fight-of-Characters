## Implemented Mechanics
1. **Concentration & The Seed of Sparda (`vergilForm` `0 -> 1`)**:
   - Builds Concentration on slashes, Summoned Swords, and parries/dashes.
   - Awakens into **Seed of Sparda** (Devil Trigger / SDT) at `<= 58%` HP or `100%` Concentration (`1.24x` speed, `1.22x` damage, accelerated cooldown ticks, demonic crimson-cobalt coat/horns, and afterimages).
2. **Yamato 5-Phase Iaijutsu Cleave**:
   - Lift -> Poised Hold -> Strike (`140°` frontal arc, `80px` reach) -> Impact Lock -> Recovery tail-to-tip crescent erase (Rules 15 & 30).
3. **Judgment Cut (`1000/1020`) & Dimension Barrage (`1150`)**:
   - Backsteps rapidly and detonates spatial slash spheres directly on target coordinates.
4. **Rapid Slash (`1050`) & Trick Teleport (`550`)**:
   - Hypersonic committed dash cleave with 4-point needle speed lines (Rule 16) and Trick ambush behind distant foes (respects Gojo Limitless Infinity).
5. **Summoned Swords (`360/1100`), Heavy Rain Swords (`2370`) & Mirage Edge Boomerang (`530`)**:
   - 6-Sword orbit ring launching homing Phantom Blades, aerial Sword Rain slow/hit barrage, and returning Force Edge boomerang.
6. **Doppelganger Clone (`1200/1220`, Base Form)**:
   - Invulnerable azure mirror clone flanking behind Vergil and amplifying Yamato cleaves (`+28%` damage).
7. **Ultimate — Judgment Cut End (`3000 -> 3004`)**:
   - Stasis lock on hostiles inside the `215px` dimensional dome (never freezing Vergil), multi-cut blitz, and glass-shatter dimension break detonation.
